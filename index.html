Welcome

Leading schools through purposeful instructional leadership

This five-day professional learning programme brought together Headmasters of Kendriya Vidyalayas at ZIET Mumbai. This digital hub brings together the course framework, day-wise proceedings, school-level planning tools, supervision resources and participant feedback.

Workshop focus: Strengthening the leadership role of Headmasters in improving teaching-learning processes and advancing excellence in the Foundational and Preparatory Stages.

Purpose

Build the capacity of school heads to lead academic improvement, support teachers and create enabling learning environments.

Course framework · 05–09 May 2026

Course Design

Instructional Leadership for Leading Excellence in Foundational & Preparatory Stage

Course objectives

  • Strengthen the instructional leadership role of Headmasters in the Foundational and Preparatory Stages.
  • Build understanding of curricular goals, competencies, learning outcomes and classroom processes.
  • Develop capacity for academic planning, supervision, assessment and teacher professional development.
  • Promote inclusive, joyful, experiential and competency-based learning.
  • Support school-level action planning and continuous improvement.

Five-day learning journey

Day 1 · 05 May
Policy to Practice – Instructional Leadership; school readiness, Balvatika, FLN and academic planning.
Day 2 · 06 May
Leading FLN & Data-Driven Schooling; curricular goals, competencies, learning outcomes and lesson planning.
Day 3 · 07 May
Assessment Reforms & Teacher Professional Development; competency-based teaching-learning and assessment.
Day 4 · 08 May
School Transformation, Governance & Innovation; computational thinking, language, assessment and multidisciplinary practices.
Day 5 · 09 May
School Governance, Innovation & Action Planning; inclusive classrooms, joyful numeracy and school-level action.
The detailed session timetable and resource-person information should be referred to the approved workshop schedule and the proceedings folder.
